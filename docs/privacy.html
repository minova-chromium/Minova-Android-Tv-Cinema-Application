Privacy notes

Your server stays
your server.

A plain-language summary of what Minova Cinema needs to connect to Plex, how it keeps that information on your device, and what the optional Android TV lighting features use.

Information stored on your device

Minova Cinema stores the Plex server address and token you provide during setup. On Android, these stay in the application’s private local preferences alongside playback preferences and a private token-free metadata and artwork cache. On Windows, saved credentials are protected with Windows user encryption; the desktop renderer does not receive the raw Plex token. Clearing application data or uninstalling removes application-owned local data, although normal operating-system backups may follow your device settings.

Network communication

The application communicates with the Plex server address you configure. Artwork, media, live playback decisions, and a short user-requested speed-test sample may be requested from that server. Plex Home and Watchlist features can communicate with services operated by Plex, Inc.; those services are governed by Plex’s own terms and privacy policy.

Minova accounts and analytics

Minova Cinema does not require a Minova account. The current application does not include an advertising SDK or a Minova-operated analytics service.

Watch state and watchlist

When you mark an item watched, resume playback, or change the Plex watchlist, the application sends that action to Plex so the state can remain consistent with other Plex clients.

Android TV cinema lighting

Cinema lighting is an optional Android TV feature and is not used by the phone, tablet, or Windows interfaces. Google Home access begins only after you choose Connect Google Home and approve access to a home. Minova reads compatible light names, rooms, device types, states, and brightness so you can choose exactly which lights Cinema Mode controls. Their identifiers stay in private app storage; Minova does not operate a server that receives this Google Home data.

TP-Link Tapo cinema lighting

Tapo integration is optional and Android TV only. Credentials are encrypted with a key held by Android Keystore and remain in private app storage. Minova uses them only to authenticate directly with compatible lights on your local network; Minova does not receive the credentials.

Local discovery broadcasts on UDP port 20002. The app reads each compatible light’s nickname, model, IP address, state, and brightness, and stores the IP addresses assigned to the Cinema Room. Disconnecting Tapo or clearing app data removes the locally stored login; uninstalling removes both the login and assignments.

Your responsibility

A Plex token and Tapo account password grant access to your devices. Keep them private, use a trusted local network, and do not include them in public bug reports. If either credential is exposed, revoke or replace it with the relevant provider.
