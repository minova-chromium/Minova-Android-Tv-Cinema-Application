Privacy notes

Your server stays
your server.

A plain-language summary of what Minova Cinema needs to connect to Plex, Google Home, and TP-Link Tapo lights, and what the application does with that information.

Information stored on the TV

Minova Cinema stores the Plex server address and token that you provide during setup in the application’s private local preferences. These values are used to authenticate requests to your Plex Media Server.

Network communication

The application communicates with the Plex server address you configure. Artwork and media may be requested from that server. Plex features can cause your Plex server or account to communicate with services operated by Plex, Inc.; those services are governed by Plex’s own terms and privacy policy.

Minova accounts and analytics

Minova Cinema does not require a Minova account. The current application does not include an advertising SDK or a Minova-operated analytics service.

Watch state and watchlist

When you mark an item watched, resume playback, or change the Plex watchlist, the application sends that action to Plex so the state can remain consistent with other Plex clients.

Google Home Cinema lighting

Google Home access is optional and begins only after you choose Connect Google Home and approve access to a home. Minova Cinema reads the names, rooms, device types, on/off states, and brightness levels of compatible lights so you can choose the exact lights used by Cinema Mode. Only lights you select are controlled. Their device identifiers are stored privately on the TV; Minova does not operate a server that receives this Google Home data.

You can change the granted home from Settings and remove Minova Cinema’s access through your Google Account’s third-party connections. Clearing the app’s data or uninstalling it removes locally stored Cinema-light assignments.

TP-Link Tapo Cinema lighting

Tapo integration is optional. The email and password you enter are encrypted using a key held by Android Keystore and remain in the app’s private storage on the TV. Minova Cinema uses them only to authenticate directly with compatible Tapo lights on your local network; Minova does not receive the credentials and the integration does not use a Minova server.

Local discovery broadcasts on UDP port 20002. The app reads each compatible light’s nickname, model, IP address, on/off state, and brightness, and stores only the IP addresses you assign to the Cinema Room in DataStore. Disconnecting Tapo or clearing the app’s data removes the locally stored login; uninstalling the app removes both the login and assignments.

Your responsibility

A Plex token and Tapo account password grant access to your devices. Keep them private, use a trusted local network, and do not include them in public bug reports. If either credential is exposed, revoke or replace it with the relevant provider.
